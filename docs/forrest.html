What would Forrest do?

2026-07-22

Instead of asking yourself: "What would Yoda [1] do?", you can also ask yourself: "What would Forrest Gump do?"

And depending on the situation, Forrest's answer might even be the wiser one [2].

Notes

[1] Or Buddha, Jesus, Gandhi, ... whoever you consider wise.

[2] The "scientific" term for this is retardmaxxing and was coined by Elisha Long [2.1] and popularized by Marc Andreessen [2.2].
