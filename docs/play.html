Play

2026-04-20

View it as play, not as competition.

When you're viewing it as competition, you focus on the short term and use tools you have mastered. You get stuck in local optima.

To become really good at something, you have to experiment, try new ways, and look stupid while doing it.

Life is a collection of infinite games. And failure in any part of it is irrelevant unless it is fatal.

So why be so serious?
