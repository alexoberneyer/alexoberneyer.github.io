Jocko has this saying about extreme ownership: take control of everything in your life. There is no one else to blame. I applied this in the past. It works. It also overshoots.

So I wrote down four checks [1] for myself:

1. **"Could I do this better?" is not "Should I do this?"**
2. **Seeing a problem is not owning it.**
3. **The required quality is not the maximum achievable quality.**
4. **Being right doesn't give you ownership.**

The hard part is following them.

## Notes

[1] Claude suggested these exact words after a long discussion. I find them helpful nonetheless.
