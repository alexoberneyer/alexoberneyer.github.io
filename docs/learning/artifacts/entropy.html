03 · Connect the mechanism

From the picture to the quantities.

The macrostate hides particle identities. Counting those hidden alternatives explains why a middle-sized split can dominate even though no individual arrangement is privileged. The bars show probability for each possible left count, not a time history. This experiment enumerates possibilities; it does not simulate gas motion.

W(N,k) = N! / [k!(N−k)!]
P(k) = W(N,k) / 2ᴺ
S / kB = ln W

N and k are dimensionless integers with 2 ≤ N ≤ 16 and 0 ≤ k ≤ N here. W is the number of arrangements. Factorial n! multiplies the integers from 1 to n; 0! = 1. ln is the natural logarithm. kB = 1.380649 × 10−23 J/K. S is the configurational contribution to entropy for this partition, in J/K.

Worked example

Label the particles A, B, C, D. The two on the left can be AB, AC, AD, BC, BD or CD. That is 6 of the 16 arrangements. P(2) = 0.375 and S/kB = ln 6 ≈ 1.791759. For all left, W = 1 and this configurational entropy is zero. Zero here does not mean the real gas has no other degrees of freedom.

Assumptions and limits

Equal-volume sides, independent positions and equal weighting are assumptions. Real gas entropy also involves momenta, indistinguishability and physical constraints. This small model allows fluctuations. It does not prove monotonic entropy in every small-system trajectory. The second law concerns total entropy and an appropriate macroscopic description, not an isolated visual impression of disorder.

04 · Follow the structure

Where else does this apply?

Gas expansion

Removing a partition opens more accessible configurations.

Boundary: Counting sides omits velocities and interactions; no temperature or expansion rate is predicted.

Binary records

Many bitstrings share one total count of ones.

Boundary: A count of bitstrings becomes a probability only with a distribution. Bits are not automatically thermodynamic J/K.

Recognition and explanation

Reveal reasoning and rubric

Each has probability 1/16. The macrostate is the union of six mutually exclusive arrangements.

Self-check: Name the level being counted and add mutually exclusive probabilities.

Calculation

Reveal reasoning and rubric

The choices are A, B or C. W = 3, probability 3/8 = 37.5%, S/kB = ln 3 ≈ 1.098612.

Self-check: Show the three arrangements and distinguish the probability from the logarithmic count.

Novel transfer

Reveal reasoning and rubric

No. Each two-left arrangement has probability 0.9² × 0.1² = 0.0081, so the macrostate has probability 0.0486. All-left has probability 0.9⁴ = 0.6561. The multiplicities have not changed; their weights have.

Self-check: Reject equal weighting, retain the count of six, and supply the changed weights.
