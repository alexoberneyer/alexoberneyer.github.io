      References: Indirect gripping, arm-drag and snap-down chapter cues refer to John Danaher’s instruction; the rear body lock reverse tai-otoshi is inspired by his Feet to Floor material. The priorities, results and transitions here reflect personal sparring observations, not a verbatim instructional or measured success-rate comparison.

      Safety: Drill at progressive resistance with a coach. For reverse tai-otoshi, break balance first, keep the calf a low barrier, and never let your extended knee take sideways load or force either partner’s knee into a twist. Ko-uchi should likewise avoid twisting a planted knee. Protect your partner on the descent.
